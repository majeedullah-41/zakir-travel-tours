General Agency FAQs

Where is Zakir Travel & Tours' Head Office located?

Our main Head Office is located at Fazal Banda Rd, Swat Matta, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan. We also operate a branch office at Office No. 7, Ground Floor, Potohar Plaza, Fazal-e-Haq Road, Blue Area, Islamabad.

Can I process my Umrah, Visa, or Air Ticket via WhatsApp or in person?

Yes! You can walk directly into our Swat Matta Head Office (or our Islamabad branch) or send your passport scans via WhatsApp to our management team (Inam Khan & Sadiq Ullah) for fast processing.
