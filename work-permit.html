FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which European country is currently open for applications?

Poland is our featured programme and is currently accepting applications. Other destinations such as Romania, Croatia, Hungary, Czech Republic and Portugal depend on live employer demand — contact our desk for the latest openings.

How long does a European work permit take?

Most programmes take approximately 2–6 months from document submission to visa decision, depending on the country, the permit authority and embassy appointment availability. Timelines shown on this page are indicative.

Can I travel to other Schengen countries with my work visa?

Yes. A valid national D-visa or residence permit from a Schengen member state generally allows short visits (up to 90 days in any 180-day period) to other Schengen countries. Your employment, however, remains tied to the issuing country.

Can I renew my permit or bring my family later?

Most EU countries allow in-country renewal of work and residence permits (for example the Karta Pobytu in Poland), and many offer family reunion after meeting income and residence conditions.

Where do I submit my original documents?
