Why Choose Poland for Work?

A Secure, Fast-Growing European Economy with Full Legal Rights

Poland has emerged as one of Central Europe's strongest industrial and logistical hubs. As a member of the European Union and Schengen Zone, a Polish National Type-D Work Visa provides international workers with legal contracts, standard social security (ZUS), and long-term career growth.

National Type-D Visa

Allows up to 1-year multiple entry stay, renewable inside Poland through Temporary Residence (Karta Pobytu).

Guaranteed Minimum Wage

Legal hourly rates set by the Polish government, paid transparently in PLN with overtime bonuses.

State Health (NFZ/ZUS)

Full medical care coverage and workplace accidental protection guaranteed under Polish labor law.

Schengen Freedom

Visit Germany, France, Italy, and 26 other Schengen member states for travel without separate tourist visas.

Frequently Asked Questions

Poland Work Permit Clarity

Honest answers to the most common questions regarding Polish work visas.

What is a Type-A Work Permit in Poland?

A Type-A Work Permit (Zezwolenie Typ A) is the standard legal document issued by a Polish Regional Voivodeship office for a foreign national working for a registered Polish employer. It serves as the primary legal basis for your National D-Type Employment Visa application.

Can I travel to other European countries on a Poland work visa?

Yes. Since Poland is an official Schengen Agreement member, a valid Polish National D-Visa permits you to travel freely throughout the 29 Schengen member states (such as Germany, France, Italy, Spain, etc.) for up to 90 days in any 180-day period for tourism and leisure while maintaining your primary employment in Poland.

Can I convert my Poland work visa into a European Residence Card (TRC)?

Yes. After arriving and commencing work in Poland, you and your employer can apply to the regional Voivode office for a Temporary Residence Card (Karta Pobytu), which grants extended residency rights and can be renewed without returning to Pakistan.

Where do I submit my original documents?

You can bring your original passport and certificates directly to our Central Head Office on 2nd Floor, Hayat Market, Matta Swat, or our secondary branch in Office No. 7, Potohar Plaza, Blue Area, Islamabad. Both offices are open Monday to Saturday for walk-in consultations.
